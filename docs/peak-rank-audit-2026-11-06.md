# OP.GG solo/duo peak audit — November 6, 2026

All 44 players and eight captains were checked against their live OP.GG profiles. Search-index copies were excluded because Yazurak’s indexed copy was older than the live page. All 52 Riot IDs matched, allowing capitalization differences. Three independent review batches agreed with the extracted maxima.

The selected value is the highest recorded solo/duo rank available across the current rank, current **Top tier**, historical within-season peaks, and historical season-end records. Flex and ranked-team queues are excluded. LP breaks ties within a tier/division. Captain draft order is unchanged.

**Coverage limit:** OP.GG does not expose within-season peaks for many older seasons. Historical season-end records prove that a rank was reached; they cannot rule out a higher unrecorded peak. The values below are the highest available records, not a claim that unavailable history was verified. This is a dated audit, not automatic synchronization.

Seven tier/division corrections were found, plus captain Ethan’s peak LP correction. Remaining entries were confirmed, with LP recorded consistently and historical seasons corrected where necessary. The companion JSON retains all extracted ranked evidence and SHA-256 hashes of the public profile HTML used.
